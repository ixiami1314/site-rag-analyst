Plans and pricing

All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Prices are per user per month and billed annually; monthly billing adds 20%.

Starter — $19 per user per month

For small teams automating their first workflows. Includes the visual Flow Builder with up to 25 action blocks per flow, 5 active flows, Flow Connect core connectors (REST, webhooks, CSV), email support, and 14 days of activity history. Minimum of 3 users.

Team — $49 per user per month

For growing operations that need the full platform. Everything in Starter, plus 120+ action blocks per flow, unlimited active flows, all 80+ Flow Connect connectors including SAP S/4HANA and Oracle NetSuite, Flow Insights dashboards and CSV/PDF exports, approval steps, version history with rollback, and priority support with a 4-hour response target. Minimum of 5 users.

Enterprise — custom

For organizations with compliance and scale requirements. Everything in Team, plus Flow Guard (SSO via SAML and OIDC, role-based access control, tamper-evident audit logs), EU data residency in Frankfurt, a dedicated success manager, a 99.9% uptime SLA, and 24/7 phone support. Custom-quoted annually based on volume.

Discounts

Registered nonprofits and educational institutions receive 40% off any plan. Annual billing effectively gives you two months free compared to monthly billing.

FAQ

Can I change plans later? Yes — upgrades apply immediately, downgrades at the end of the billing period.

What happens after the trial? Your workspace switches to read-only until you pick a plan; flows and history are kept for 90 days.

What counts as a user

A user is anyone who logs in — operators, approvers, designers, auditors. System-to-system calls through the API are not users; they are covered by the plan's rate limits. You can deactivate a seat at any time and it stops billing at the end of the month.

Billing details

Plans bill annually by default (two months effectively free versus monthly). We accept bank transfer and all major cards; Enterprise invoices are issued with net-30 terms. VAT applies for EU customers unless a valid VAT number is provided. Prices are listed in US dollars; EUR and GBP invoices are available on request.

Plan comparison notes

The 25-action-block limit on Starter covers simple linear flows with one or two branches; most teams outgrow it within a quarter and move to Team. Version history with rollback is a Team feature — Starter keeps the current version only. Flow Guard (SSO, RBAC, audit logs, EU residency) is available on Enterprise only because it provisions workspace-wide controls.
