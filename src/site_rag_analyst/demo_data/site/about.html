About Meridian Flow

Meridian Flow B.V. builds workflow automation for logistics teams. The company was founded in 2019 in Rotterdam by Mara Jansen (CEO) and Tom de Vries (CTO), who met while digitizing customs paperwork at a freight forwarder and decided the tools should be built once, properly.

Team and offices

We are 64 people across two offices: the headquarters at Moerstraat 24 in Rotterdam, and an engineering hub in Munich opened in 2022. About half the team works in engineering and solutions; the rest spans customer success, sales and operations.

Investors

In 2024 we raised a €12 million Series B led by Harbor Ventures with participation from returning seed investors. The round funds our EU expansion and the Flow Guard compliance roadmap. Earlier rounds: a €1.5M pre-seed (2020) and a €5M Series A (2022).

How we work

We ship every two weeks, publish the changelog publicly, and support customers directly — engineers join support rotation so the people who build the platform hear what breaks. Our customer base is more than 300 logistics organizations across the EU and UK.

Values we actually use

Three of them. Own the border — problems get names, owners and deadlines, not committees. Boring is a feature — logistics runs on trust; we prefer dull, predictable releases to clever ones. Answer for it — engineers join support rotation, and the changelog is public because hiding it would be easier for us, not better for you.

Working here

We hire remotely inside the EU and at both offices. Interviews are practical: a systems conversation, a code or process review with the team, and a paid trial week for final-round candidates. Open roles are listed on the contact page.
