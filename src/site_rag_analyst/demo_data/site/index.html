Automate the paperwork between shipments

Meridian Flow is a workflow automation platform built for logistics teams. Freight forwarders and customs brokers use it to connect their systems, design routing workflows visually, and clear exceptions before they become delays.

Why teams choose Meridian Flow

Every shipment generates paperwork: bookings, customs declarations, delivery confirmations, invoices. Most of it is still re-typed between systems by hand. Meridian Flow moves that work to automated flows that run exactly the way your operators already work — with approvals where humans matter.

How it works

Meridian Flow sits between your systems. Connectors pull shipment data in, flows apply your routing and approval rules, and finished documents land wherever they belong — a portal, an ERP, or an email inbox. Operators watch everything on the run timeline and step in exactly where the flow asks them to.

  • Connect — link SAP, NetSuite, EDI mailboxes, carrier portals and shared inboxes once.
  • Design — clone a template, adjust steps on the canvas, publish a new version.
  • Operate — approvals arrive with full context; exceptions alert the right person immediately.
  • Measure — Insights shows cycle times, SLA attainment and anomalies per customer.
