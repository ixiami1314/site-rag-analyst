Contact and support

We keep support close to the product: the engineers who build Meridian Flow also answer for it.

Support channels

  • Email: support@meridianflow.test — answered for all paying plans; Starter within 2 business days, Team within 4 business hours.
  • Phone: +31 10 555 0182 — 24/7 for Enterprise SLA customers with a named hotline; otherwise weekdays.
  • In-app chat — available on Team and Enterprise plans during support hours.

Support hours

Standard support hours are 9:00 to 17:00 Central European Time, Monday to Friday, excluding Dutch public holidays. Enterprise customers with a 24/7 SLA reach an on-call engineer by phone at any hour, with a 15-minute acknowledgement target for critical incidents.

Offices

Rotterdam headquarters: Moerstraat 24, 3011 Rotterdam, Netherlands. Munich engineering hub: Sendlinger Straße 8, 80331 München, Germany. Visitors are welcome by appointment.

Sales and partnerships

For volume pricing, EDI migrations or partnership questions, email sales@meridianflow.test or use the demo request form on the plans page. We reply within one business day.

Status

Live system status and incident history are published at status.meridianflow.test, with email and RSS subscription.

Support tiers at a glance

  • Starter — email support, response within 2 business days, support hours 9:00-17:00 CET.
  • Team — email plus in-app chat, response within 4 business hours, priority queue.
  • Enterprise — 24/7 phone hotline with 15-minute acknowledgement on critical incidents, named success manager, quarterly service review.

Reporting a security issue

Email security@meridianflow.test (PGP key on the Flow Guard page). We acknowledge within one business day, triage within three, and run a coordinated disclosure process with bounty rewards for confirmed vulnerabilities.

Partners and EDI migrations

Implementation partners handle EDI migrations and legacy decommissioning programs; the partner directory lists certified firms by region. For partnership inquiries email partners@meridianflow.test.
