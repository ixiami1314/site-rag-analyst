Flow Insights

Flow Insights turns the events your flows generate into operational analytics: where shipments wait, which steps breach SLA, and how throughput trends over time.

Cycle-time analytics

Every step of every flow is timed automatically. Dashboards break cycle time down by stage, lane, customer and carrier, so you can see that customs clearance averages 18 hours for one customer but 41 for another — and fix the difference.

SLA tracking

Define SLA targets per flow or per customer — for example "booking confirmation within 2 hours of arrival". Insights tracks attainment daily, flags breaches as they approach, and attributes each breach to the step that caused it.

Anomaly alerts

Baseline models learn the normal volume and cycle time per flow. When a flow suddenly runs three times slower — an integration outage, a stuck approval — Insights raises an anomaly alert to the channel you choose: email, Slack, or the mobile app.

Exports

Any dashboard or table exports to CSV or PDF, either on demand or on a schedule (daily, weekly, monthly). Scheduled exports can land in S3 buckets or email inboxes. CSV export shipped in version 2.2.0.

Dashboards

Dashboards are grids of widgets — cycle-time histograms, SLA attainment gauges, throughput trend lines, top exception reasons, per-operator approval latency. Build them per team or per customer; every widget drills down to the underlying runs with one click, so a number is never a dead end.

Data retention

Insights retains event-level data for 24 months and aggregates indefinitely. Enterprise workspaces can extend event retention to 7 years to match their audit-log policy. Nothing is sampled — the numbers are computed from the same events the audit log records.

Sharing

Share a dashboard three ways: a permalink for teammates, a scheduled PDF to stakeholders, or an embed token for your intranet. Permalinks inherit role permissions — an auditor sees the SLA widget but not the document preview inside it.
